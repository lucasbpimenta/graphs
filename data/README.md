# Data

## Raw data (`raw/`)

Files downloaded from the ANP website in October 2026 and kept unchanged. The CSV files are in Latin-1 with `;` as separator.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `relacao-concessionarios.csv` | Contracts in force: contract × concessionaire, CNPJ, share, operator, phase, operating environment. All auctions (Round Zero to the third Permanent Offer cycle under production sharing). 883 contracts. |
| `processos-cessao-contratos.csv` | Approved rights assignments, 2001–2026: composition before and after, assignor, assignee, approval date. |
| `blocos-ofertados-rodadas.csv` | Blocks offered and awarded in Rounds 9 to 17, with water depth. |
| `anp_resultados/ofertas-vencedoras-rodadas-1-17.xlsx` | Composition of the winning consortia in Rounds 1 to 17 (the eighth round was annulled), one sheet per round. |
| `anp_resultados/blocos-ofertados-rodadas-1-17.xlsx` | Blocks offered in Rounds 1 to 17, with location (water depth). |
| `anp_resultados/metadado-processos-cessao-contratos.pdf` | Data dictionary of the assignment file (codes of `tipo_solicitacao`). |
| `anp_opc/planilha-blocos-em-oferta-opc-v04.xlsx` | Blocks of the Permanent Offer under concession, with sector and operating environment. |
| `anp_opc/relatorio-*.pdf` | Judgment reports of cycles 4 and 5 of the Permanent Offer, with the sector of each awarded block. |
| `anp_opc/setores_blocos_opc4_opc5.csv` | Block → sector for the blocks awarded in cycles 4 and 5, generated by `scripts/00_setores_opc.py`. |
| `preferencia_petrobras.csv` | Compiled by the authors: production-sharing areas in which Petrobras could exercise the preferential right of Law 13,365/2016, whether it did, and how it entered the winning consortium (`entrada_petrobras`), with the CNPE resolution and the source document of each row. |

## Harmonized base (`processed/`)

Generated by `scripts/01_base_harmonizada.py`. All files are UTF-8, one row per company in each consortium.

| File | Unit | Main columns |
|---|---|---|
| `participacoes_atuais.csv` | contract × company | `contrato`, `chave`, `bid`, `regime`, `ano_leilao`, `area`, `bacia`, `ambiente` (`Mar` = offshore, `Terra` = onshore), `fase`, `cnpj`, `empresa`, `grupo`, `participacao`, `operador`, `lamina`, `fonte_lamina` |
| `ofertas_vencedoras.csv` | (round, block) × company | `rodada`, `ano_leilao`, `bacia`, `bloco`, `localizacao`, `bonus`, `empresa`, `grupo`, `participacao`, `operador`, `n_empresas` |
| `cessoes.csv` | assignment × situation (before/after) × company | `registro`, `tipo_solicitacao`, `contrato`, `data_aprovacao`, `situacao`, `empresa`, `grupo`, `participacao`, `operador`, `cedente`, `cessionaria` |
| `empresas_aliases.csv` | raw company name | `nome_bruto`, `empresa`, `regra`, `grupo_atual` |

`lamina` (offshore contracts only) is `rasa` (shallow, 0–400 m) or `profunda` (deep or ultra-deep water), the cut-off used in ANP statistics. `fonte_lamina` records the source of the classification, in this order of priority: `pre_sal_legal` (production-sharing areas, which by law lie in the pre-salt polygon), `blocos_ofertados` (location of the block in the offered-block files or in the Permanent Offer sector) and `cessoes` (operating environment reported in the assignments). Rounds 1 to 4 have no water-depth information.

`chave` is the contract number normalized as `NNNNNN/YYYY[S]`, which links the portfolio to the assignments.

`cessoes.tipo_solicitacao`, as defined in the ANP data dictionary: 1 = total or partial transfer of rights; 2 = merger, split or incorporation that changes the concessionaire; 3 = change of operator; 4 = exemption or replacement of performance guarantee; 5 = division of the area. Codes can be combined ("1 e 3").

### Regimes (`participacoes_atuais.regime`)

`rodada_zero` (Round Zero, 1998), `rodada_concessao` (concession Rounds 1–17), `oferta_permanente_concessao` (Permanent Offer, concession), `acumulacoes_marginais` (marginal accumulations, onshore only), `partilha_rodada` (production-sharing rounds 1–6), `oferta_permanente_partilha` (Permanent Offer, production sharing), `cessao_onerosa` (transfer of rights, 2010), `excedentes_cessao_onerosa` (transfer-of-rights surplus), `outros` (other areas). In the offshore networks (`scripts/redes_mar.py`) they are grouped into the layers concession, Permanent Offer (concession), production sharing (rounds, Permanent Offer and surplus), Round Zero and others.

### Companies and economic groups

`empresa` is the same company across changes of name (Statoil → Equinor, Esso → ExxonMobil, HRT → PRIO, QGEP → Enauta). `grupo` is the controlling economic group in the year of the event; mergers and acquisitions between different companies are dated in `scripts/empresas.py` (`EVENTOS`), for example BG → Shell (2016), Maersk Oil → TotalEnergies (2018), 3R and Enauta → Brava Energia (2024). In `participacoes_atuais.csv`, `grupo` is the current group. The offshore networks of the paper use the current group in both the award and the current network, so that corporate mergers do not count as changes of partners.

### Composition at award (`scripts/02_redes_regime.py`)

`composicao_adjudicacao.csv` gives the composition of each contract in force at award. The column `fonte` records the source, in this order of priority: `leilao_oficial` (block found in the official Round 1–17 results), `cessao_antes` (composition "before" the first approved assignment), `atual_sem_cessao` (no assignment ever registered, so the current composition is the one at award) and `rodada_zero` (Petrobras with 100%). In Rounds 9 to 17, the reconstruction from the first assignment matches the official result in 81 of 84 contracts, including the shares.

The files `arestas_*.csv` are edge lists with `peso` = number of contracts held jointly; `centralidades_*.csv` hold the centrality measures. The suffix `_grupo` indicates the level of economic groups and `_mar` the offshore contracts.

## Known issues in the source data

In the assignment file, 13 of the 2,294 before/after situations have shares that do not add up to 100% and 5 entries have no share; they were kept as published. Round Zero contracts were signed by Petrobras alone on 6 August 1998; partnerships formed afterwards appear only through assignments registered from 2001.
